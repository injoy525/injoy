What matters in 2026

Growth opportunities and risks can exist at the same time.

Growth drivers

1

AI data centres

AI training and inference require advanced processors, networking chips and more computing capacity.

2

Memory and HBM

High-bandwidth memory is important for moving data quickly to AI processors; memory is a major part of the revised 2026 forecast.

3

Advanced packaging

Putting compute and memory closer together can improve system performance and energy efficiency.

Risks to watch

!

AI investment slows

If customers delay data-centre projects or AI revenue disappoints, future chip demand could weaken.

!

Supply and price swings

Memory shortages can raise costs; if producers expand too much, a later oversupply could push prices down.

!

Uneven recovery

AI-related chips may be strong while some consumer, PC, smartphone or automotive segments recover more slowly.

Forecast vs actual: quick guide

Use this checklist when reading industry news.

  • Actual / reported: a period has ended and an organization has published its sales figure, such as SIA's 2025 annual total or Q2 2026 quarterly sales.
  • Forecast / projected: an estimate about a period that has not finished, such as the WSTS full-year 2026 forecast.
  • Estimate / outlook: an analyst's expectation or scenario; the method and publication date may differ from another forecast.
  • Do not mix revisions: WSTS's earlier 2025 forecast for 2026 was $975B, while its Spring 2026 forecast rose to $1.51T. These are different forecast vintages, not two actual results.
