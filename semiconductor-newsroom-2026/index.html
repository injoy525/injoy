AI chip + memory: the chef analogy

A simple way to understand why both compute and memory matter

👨‍🍳 ⚡ ⇄ 🥬 🚚

The AI processor is the chef. It performs calculations and works on the task. High-bandwidth memory (HBM) is the fast ingredient conveyor. It feeds the processor the data it needs.

If the conveyor is too slow or too small, the chef waits—even if the chef is very fast. That is why AI systems need both powerful processors and memory with high bandwidth.

Why memory is in focus

Industry view from Deloitte and KPMG

AI training and inference need fast movement of large amounts of data. HBM is designed to deliver high bandwidth close to AI accelerators, while demand for server memory can compete with other devices for manufacturing capacity.

Memory prices and capacity can move in cycles. Strong demand may help suppliers, but shortages can raise costs for PCs, phones and other electronics.

Risk monitor

Key issues highlighted by KPMG and Deloitte; these are risks to watch, not predictions that each will occur.

🌐 Tariffs & trade policy

Policy changes can alter costs, market access and where companies choose to manufacture.

🔌 Energy & infrastructure

Factories and AI data centers require reliable power, water, cooling and grid capacity.

🏭 Cost of new fabs

Advanced factories need very large investments and can take years to build and ramp up.

📦 Supply-chain bottlenecks

Advanced packaging, HBM and specialized tools can limit output even when chip demand is strong.

📉 AI spending slowdown

If customers cannot earn enough from AI services, data-center projects or chip orders may be delayed.

👩‍🔬 Skills & talent

Companies need experienced engineers and technicians to expand production and develop new processes.

Sources & methodology

Start with the primary research, then compare it with independent reporting.

  • World Semiconductor Trade Statistics (WSTS) — Autumn 2025 forecast: worldwide market size and growth outlook.
  • KPMG / Global Semiconductor Alliance — 2026 outlook: survey of 151 executives and their expectations, growth drivers and concerns.
  • Deloitte — 2026 Global Semiconductor Industry Outlook: market forecast, AI chip demand, memory and investment risks.
  • Reuters — TSMC Q3 2026 revenue report (8 Oct 2026): company-specific reported results.

Method: Forecasts, surveys, and reported company results are labeled separately. Market totals, company revenue, and survey percentages measure different things and should not be compared as if they were the same metric. Figures may be revised after publication.
